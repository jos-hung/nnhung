## Problem

Mobile devices generate tasks that must finish before strict deadlines. Deciding which tasks to offload to an edge server, and in what order to execute them, is a combinatorial problem where a single bad decision can cause cascading deadline misses.

## Approach

- Formulated joint task scheduling and offloading under hard deadline constraints.
- Designed **greedy algorithms with correctness and performance analysis**, and compared them with **learning-based (DRL)** approaches.
- The scheduling method was filed as a patent: *Job Scheduling with Deadline Constraints* (with Prof. Sang-Woon Jeon).
