**Where:** AI Center, FPT Software, Hanoi · 2024

## What I did

- Applied **quantization-aware training (QAT)** and **post-training quantization (PTQ)** to image classification and object detection models, including ResNet, SSD-ResNet and DETR-based models.
- Built quantized inference pipelines and deployed them on CPU with **Apache TVM**.
- Benchmarked latency, efficiency and accuracy trade-offs to assess deployment feasibility.
